# Family History (private genealogy section)

The owner's family research, served to the family members whose accounts are matched to a person in the family tree. THIS REPOSITORY IS PUBLIC: no real names, tree ids, findings or family data may ever be committed (code, docs, tests, fixtures). All family data lives only in the private bucket. Page `/family-history`; API `/api/kade/family-history`. Started 29 Sep 2026; v2 (the iPhone app's routes) the same evening.

## The owner's decisions (29 Sep 2026)
- **Who gets in:** only accounts matched to a person in the tree ("blood and married-in family"), the owner, and guests she adds one by one (a guest sees the tree from her place, in her words). Close friends in the Family feature pack do NOT get in unless she adds them.
- **The family sees the research as she does**, from their own place in the tree: living relatives in full (dates, places, records, photos), research notes, DNA findings and whatever the export sends about DNA matches. The server takes nothing out for matched family; what the export sends is what they see.
- **Sensitive research findings** (who a father was, found by DNA) are visible to every matched family member, always marked as research, never featured on Home, On this day, the reel or in the game, and opened behind a heads-up. `KADE_FH_DNA_FINDINGS` stays as an emergency switch (below).
- **Children** see the same as adults.
- **Strangers, test seats and the App Review seat** never get in; each is told why, in words a greyed Library row can show.

## Data (built offline from the owner's archive, uploaded to B2)
Bucket `KADE_MEDIA_BUCKET || AWS_BUCKET_NAME`, prefix `KADE_FAMILY_HISTORY_PREFIX || 'family-history'`.
- `<prefix>/current.json` = `{"version": "20260101-120000"}` (the only object that is ever overwritten).
- `<prefix>/<version>/bundle.json.gz`: gzip JSON:
  - `version`, `generated`, `owner` (the owner's tree id), `counts`, `anchors` (tree ids that have a view file)
  - `people[id]`: `id, name, label, sex, birthSurname, lifespan, living, virtual, confidence, birth{date,place}, death{date,place}, burial{date,place}, otherNames[], facts[{type,label,date,year,place,value,note,records[key]}], parents[{id,kind: birth|step|probable|doubtful|adopted}], spouses[id], children[id], records[key], wrongRecords{key: why}, memorials[mid], wrongMemorials{mid: why}, media[mediaId], notes[], history[], duplicateOf, duplicateWhy, conflict, ownerRelation, ownerGroup`
  - `records[key]` (key = "collectionId:recordId"): `key, collection, name, fields[[label,value]], tables[[[cells]]], citation, url, image (mediaId|null)`; optional `evidenceWarning`, `sourceExcerpt`, `sourceExcerptCoverage`, `sourceCitation`, `sourceUrl`, `newspaperSource`. Saved excerpts and actual recovery citations stay separate from the originally requested record; partial cached listings have no fabricated image.
  - `memorials[mid]`: `id, name, birth_date, birth_place, death_date, death_place, cemetery, cemetery_place, plot, inscription, bio, url, family{heading:[{memorial_id,name,dates}]}, photos[{media, caption}], family_notes[]`
  - `media[mediaId]`: `id, kind (record|grave|tree|web|restored), file ("media/<id>.<ext>", relative to the prefix), caption, people[], bytes, date?, place?, description?, media_kind?, heldFor?`
  - `findings[{summary, people[]}]`, `stories[{slug,title,file,words}]` (file relative to the version folder, markdown), `sources{sid:{title}}`, `codexSources[...]`
- **Export v2 (`schema: 2`)** adds, every part optional (the routes work on a v1 bundle and use each part as it arrives):
  - people: `portrait` (the best picture's id), `withheld` (a count), `research` (`dna|guess` for research-only people), `presumedLiving`, `mayBeLiving`
  - media: `category` (portrait|photo|record|grave|document|story), `w`, `h`, `sizes` and `sizeFiles{t|s|l|f|o: {file, w, h, bytes}}` (t 400 px, s 2,048, l 4,096 for big scans, f a 256 px face crop, o a scan's original; a photo's `file` becomes its metadata-free `s` copy), `alt`, `description`, `text`, `textAuto`, `isPortrait`, `faces[{x,y,w,h}]` (fractions), `describedBy: "automatic"`, `textFile`/`hasText` for story documents; a restored copy is its own entry (`kind: "restored"`, `restoredFrom`, `restoredNotes`, `faithful`) and its original carries `restored: <id>`
  - `findings[]` gain `key, title, text, proof (records|dna|guess), band, sensitive, dna, evidence, storySlug`; `sensitivePeople[]`
  - `stories[]` gain `short[3], research, sensitive, people[] (Who's who), findings[], minutes`
  - `milestones[{md, year, date, type: birth|death|marriage, people[], placeText}]` (people who have died, full dates)
  - `changes[{version, date, since, people[], updated[], media[]}]` (the last 20 exports)
  - `dna{tested[], testSide, summary, clusters[{key, title, couple[2], members, band, proof, text, matches?[]}], conclusions[{key, title, text, proof, band, sensitive, people[], clusters[], storySlug}], details{title, rows[], caveats[]}}`
  - `places{ready, places[{id, short, lat, lon, precision: town|county|state|country, state, country}] (at most 80), stays[{p, pl, y, t}], moves[{p, from, to, y}], ocean[{p, from, to, born, y, country}], unplaced[], note}`
- `<prefix>/<version>/views/<id without @>.json.gz`: `{anchor, relations: {personId: {term, group: self|ancestor|descendant|blood|marriage, gen?, distance?, path?[ids from the anchor up to the shared ancestor(s) and down], pathText?, notes?[]}}}`. v2 relations add `cor, ahnen[], side (father|mother|both|marriage), kind (full|half), research (dna|guess)`; v2 views add `follows, coverage, paper[], birthplaces[], abroad[], faces[]`. A person missing from `relations` has no known link to the anchor.
- `<prefix>/media/<file>`: pictures, scans and size files. Media files are NOT versioned (same id = same file).
- Written by the server: `<prefix>/inbox/<time>-<random>.json` (notes to the owner) and `<prefix>/audio/<story hash>/<part hash>.{wav,json}` (Listen).

## Access rule (server-enforced)
`familyHistoryViewer(user, bundle, ownerUserId = KADE_FH_OWNER_USER_ID)` returns `{personId, mode}` or null:
1. review seat (`libraryReviewSeat`) or test seat (`libraryTestSeat`) → null, whatever else the account carries.
2. **Owner mode:** when `KADE_FH_OWNER_USER_ID` is set, only the ADMIN account with that id (seen from `bundle.owner`). While it is unset, an ADMIN who is not matched to someone else (the v1 rule), so the owner is never shut out before the setting is made.
3. `user.kadeFamilyTreePerson` set and present in `bundle.people` → that person (mode `family`; a duplicate entry becomes its `duplicateOf`).
4. `user.kadeFamilyHistory === 'guest'` → the owner's place (mode `guest`, the owner's words: "Ada's grandmother"). An unrelated administrator receives no implicit guest access.
5. otherwise null.
If a family member's person has no view file, the owner's view is used, in the owner's words (`viewNote`). The owner pages (`/accounts`, `/match`, `/notes`) answer the owner account only (any ADMIN while `KADE_FH_OWNER_USER_ID` is unset); never the review seat or a test seat.
User schema fields (packages/data-schemas, beside kadeLibraryAccess): `kadeFamilyTreePerson: String`, `kadeFamilyHistory: 'guest'|'none'`, `kadeFamilyHistoryAskedAt: Date`.

**Server settings, read on every request:** `KADE_FH_OWNER_USER_ID` (the owner's account id; set on the server, never in this repository), `KADE_FH_DNA_FINDINGS` = `family` (default: every matched family member sees sensitive findings and stories), `owner` (the owner alone) or `off` (nobody); guests never. `KADE_FH_LISTEN_VOICE` / `KADE_FH_LISTEN_STEER` override the Library voice and direction for Listen (new audio is made for a new voice).

## API (all `requireJwtAuth`, JSON, `Cache-Control: no-store`)
**Refusals.** Every route answers a refused account `403 {access: false, reason: review|test|unmatched|declined, error, detail, hint, canAsk, askedAt}`, e.g. `{reason: "unmatched", detail: "Not linked to the tree yet", hint: "Ask the tree's owner to match your account.", canAsk: true}`; after an ask, `detail: "Asked on 5 March 2026"` and `canAsk: false` for a week. A stranger never costs a bucket read. A bundle that cannot load answers `503 {error: "The family history is being updated. Try again in a minute."}`.

**`POST /ask`** (an account that is not matched): `{ok, askedAt, text: "Asked. The tree's owner will see your request."}`; 429 within a week of the last ask; 403 for the review seat, test seats, `declined`, and accounts that can already open it. `/accounts` lists askers first with `askedAt`; `/match` clears it.

### v1 (legacy answers)
- `GET /me` → `{access: true, viewer: {personId, name, label, relationToOwner, first, inTree}, mode, isOwner, version, counts, owner: {first}, row: {detail, hint}, viewNote?}`. `viewer.first` greets by the account's own name (never the owner's for a guest); `row.detail` is the Library row's words ("Ada's sister", "Your tree", "Guest").
- `GET /person/:id` → `{person (+ relation), family: {parents, spouses, children, siblings}, records (+ wrong), memorials (+ photos, wrong), media, findings}`; 404 for unknown ids. Living relatives' records are served like anyone's.
- `GET /tree?focus=<id>&up=4&down=2` (caps up 8, down 4) → `{focus, up, down, nodes, links, couples}`.
- `GET /search?q=` → up to 50 `{id, label, lifespan, relation?}`, relatives first.
- `GET /people?group=ancestor|blood|marriage|all` → `{id, label, lifespan, relation, group, gen?, distance?}` nearest first.
- `GET /stories` → `[{slug, title, words}]`; `GET /story/:slug` → `{slug, title, markdown}`.
- `GET /findings` → `[{summary, people}]`.
- `GET /media/:id` → `{url}` (signed for an hour; `?redirect=1` → 302). With `?size=t|s|l|f|o` → `{url, w, h, expires}` from the export's own size files; `o` only for record and document scans, never a photo (a phone's original can carry GPS). Pictures the export held for a few people (`heldFor`) answer 404 to everyone else.
- Owner only: `GET /accounts` → `[{userId, name, username, personId, personLabel, access, testSeat, changeable, askedAt}]`; `POST /match {userId, personId|null, guest?}`.

### v2 (the website and iPhone app)
Every visible and spoken word is written on the server (`words.ts`); no `spoken` field contains a dash, middle dot, arrow or shouted word (a test runs over every route). Shared shapes:
- **Person card:** `{id, name, first, years ("1850–1921"), yearsSpoken ("1850 to 1921"), living, gen, term ("your 2nd great-grandmother"), chain ("your mom's mom's dad", up to three steps), side (father|mother|both|marriage|self|descendant), sideText ("Mom's side"), research ({level: dna|guess, text} | null), face ({id, face, thumb, alt, showing} | null), initials, spoken}`.
- **Image:** `{id, category, w, h, year, sizes[], thumb, face, short, alt, description, text, date, place, described ("auto"|null), hasText, textAuto, faces[], shareable (always), shareName, restored (the restored copy's id), restoredLabel ("Restored with AI: colours and repairs may be guessed"), showing (original|restored), original}`. Full photographs prefer the prepared restored copy, with the original selectable and AI estimates clearly labelled. Named person cards use only original face crops with `portraitPersonId` matching the person and `portraitIdentityBasis: "reviewed-face-identity"`; neither a detected face nor a single tree attachment establishes identity. Groups without a reviewed crop remain complete gallery images. Records and documents are never restored. Before the export makes sizes, `thumb` is null and `GET /media/:id` still signs the stored file.
- **Proof words, one scale:** "Proven by records", "Strong DNA evidence (about 90 to 95% sure)", "Best guess"; research is always spoken as "research finding, … not proven by records".
- **Tile:** `{key, title, detail, spoken, hint, enabled, reason, open: {to, id?, since?, filter?}}`.

Routes:
- `GET /home?since=<version>` → `{version, mode, isOwner, hero {hello, headline, youAre, stats, follows, spoken, open}, faces {layout: row|portrait|none, people[], portrait}, reel {title, detail, cover, cards[you|grandparents|oldest|ocean|mystery|places|end]}, featured[onThisDay|ancestorOfWeek|story] (≤3), news, tiles[tree, photos, whereWhen, dna], more[stories, discoveries, mysteries?, people, play, note], comingSoon, footnote, owner {notes, asks} | null}`. Faces, featured, news and the reel's history cards never show anyone on a research path or in a sensitive finding; featured and history cards show people who have died.
- `GET /tree?v=2` adds `layout {boxes[{key "<id>#<n>", id, gen, row, x, role, repeat, you, person, moreAbove, spoken}], edges[{from, to, kind}], couples, width, rows, order, extraParents}` in box units (a port of the web page's layout, checked box for box), `summary`, `legend`, and `list` (generation headings with rows).
- `GET /person/:id?v=2` adds `person` card fields, `bornA`, `duplicate`, `header`/`headerKind`, `nutshell`, `livedThrough`, `relation {term, chain, ladder, pathPeople, pathText, dnaLine}`, `pictures {total, items ≤20}` (restored first), `life[]`, family cards with `kindText`, `records[]` with `title, image (the scan as an Image; v1's media id is kept as imageId), household, spoken, wrongText`, `grave`, `findings` (cards), `sources`, `withheld`, `share`.
- `GET /gallery?kind=photos|portraits|records|graves|documents|stories|all&person&since&sort=near|year&from` → `{kind, title, total, from, count, kinds[], items[Image + caption, people, index], prev, next, pageSpoken}` (pages of 48).
- `POST /media/sign {ids: [≤100], size}` → `{urls: {id: url}, size, expires}` (unknown, held or missing sizes left out).
- `GET /media/:id/info` → `{image, caption, evidenceWarning, newspaperSource, description, described, describedNote, text (saved source words or a story document's text file), textAuto, textNote, restoredNotes, people, source: {kind,title,citation,url}, canAskRestore}`. The source citation belongs to the saved original; a prepared variant's visual description belongs to the selected copy. URLs are restricted to HTTP(S). `evidenceWarning` requests an identity review; it does not imply an uncertain namesake is confirmed wrong. `newspaperSource` has optional `coverage`, `indexedPersonRole`, `principalArticleSubject`, `identityReview: {status,note}`, `linkedTreeIdentityVerified`, `requestedSourceUrl`, actual `sourceUrl`, `citation`, and `limitations[]`. Person records retain these fields, read saved excerpts without a provider membership, and distinguish the subject of an article from the indexed relative.
- `GET /dna?for=<id>` → `{title, for, forName, follows, test, paper, birthplaces, abroad, compare}`. `test` is for the signed-in viewer only: `applies` self (the owner), fullSibling, halfSibling (sharing the parent on the tested side), sharedLine (only the families they descend from); null for guests and people married in. Cards carry the cluster's `band`, `members` and `matches` when the export sends them; `mysteries` (sensitive conclusions, behind `headsUp`) follow `KADE_FH_DNA_FINDINGS`; `details {title, rows, caveats, clusters}`. `for` may be only the viewer, a spouse or a child (400 otherwise) and changes only `paper` (generations 1 to 7, wedges by ahnentafel number with `state: known|research|unknown`; a half fan when the tree follows one side), `birthplaces` (counts by state or country, never percentages) and `abroad`. `compare.averages` are shares on paper; `details` for cM stays null until a checked copy of the published Shared cM table is added.
- `GET /timeline?scope=ancestors|all` → `{scope, title, top, hint, follows, lanes, mapReady, people {id: card}, decades[{decade, title, summary, spoken, photo, bars[{id, lane, from, to, side, research}], context[{key, title, text, spoken}], events[{year, text, spoken, personId, research}]}]}`, newest first; `all` includes living relatives, their lifelines running to today.
- `GET /places?scope=` → `{places[], people, start, decades[{decade, title, summary, spoken, counts {placeId: n}, labels (top 5), moves[], list[{heading, rows[{placeId, text, people}]}]}], ocean[{personId, text, from, to}], journeys[{personId, text}], unplaced, note}`; `404 {error: "The map is coming soon.", missing: "places"}` until the export has a map.
- `GET /play?count=5&seed=` → `{seed, rounds[{kind: relation|side|older|year|birthplace, prompt, spoken, people, image, choices[{text, spoken}], answer, explain, spokenExplain, right, wrong}], score}`; the same seed plays the same game; `403 {reason: "guest"}` for guests.
- `GET /search?v=2&q=` → `{query, total, text ("12 people found"), spoken, people[card]}`.
- `GET /people?v=2&group=ancestor|blood|marriage|all&from=` → `{group, title, total, from, count, prev, next, pageSpoken, people[card], sections[{heading, level, rows[{id, spoken, person}]}]}` (pages of 60; ancestors under generation headings; blood includes children).
- `GET /stories?v=2` → `{stories[{slug, title, words, detail, research}], clippings[{id, title, people}]}`.
- `GET /story/:slug?v=2` adds `{detail, research {banner}, short, whoswho[card], blocks[{type: h2|h3|p|li|quote, runs[{text, em?, strong?, link?, source?}], n?}], sources[{n, title, url}], chunks[{i, text, cues[{text, start, end}] (fractions), audio ("/story/<slug>/audio/<i>")}], listen}`. Source paths become numbered chips with plain titles and never reach a reader.
- `GET /findings?v=2` → `{discoveries[Finding], mysteries {title, headsUp, count} | null}`; `?group=mysteries` → `{title, headsUp, available, findings[]}`. Finding: `{key, title, text, summary, proof, proofText, proofSpoken, people[card], evidence, storySlug, dna, spoken}`.

**Listen** (stories read aloud in the Library's voice):
- `GET /story/:slug/listen` → `{slug, title, listen, count, parts[{i, text, cues, audio: "/story/<slug>/audio/<i>", ready}]}` (parts cut like the Library reader: whole sentences, about 450 characters, never over 600).
- `GET /story/:slug/audio/:i` → `{slug, i, count, text, mime, duration (s), url (signed an hour), expires, cues[{text, start, end}] (seconds), next}`; `?redirect=1` → 302. A part is voiced the first time anyone asks, through the Reading Room's proxy, default voice and narrator direction, and kept under `<prefix>/audio/<story hash>/<part hash>` (hashes of the words and the voice, never a title); after that it is only signed. The next part is voiced ahead; two at a time at most; never twice. 502 when the voice does not answer, 503 when Listen is not set up.

**Notes to the owner:**
- `POST /note {kind: memory|who|restore-request, about: {personId}|{mediaId}|null, text (≤ 2,000)}` → `{ok, id, text: "Sent to Ada. Thank you."}`; `who` needs a picture; `restore-request` needs a photo that is not a record or document and not already restored (its text defaults to "Please restore this photo"). Anyone let in may send one, at most 20 a day.
- Owner only: `GET /notes` → `[{id, at, from {userId, name}, kind, kindText, about {person}|{image}|null, text, done, doneAt}]` newest first; `POST /notes/:id/done {done?: false}`.

## Separate authorized archives

Set `KADE_FH_OWNER_USER_ID` to the verified owner account for production. Once set, an unrelated administrator has no implicit family access: only the configured ADMIN owner, a checked person binding, or an explicitly authorized one-by-one guest grant can enter. Family-pack membership does not grant history access. Before this setting is configured, the legacy administrator-owner fallback remains for initial setup; do not publish a new pointer until the verified owner setting and actual audience have been checked.

`familyHistoryArchivesRouter` wraps the existing router. With no `KADE_FH_ARCHIVES` setting, all existing paths and access rules remain unchanged. The private setting is a JSON array of `{id,title,prefix,ownerUserId,members:[{userId,personId?}]}`. Use verified account IDs and verified tree identities only; omitting a member's personId makes that explicit reader a guest with the owner's relationships and without sensitive findings. The configured owner must be the verified ADMIN owner account to use owner controls. Never commit real bindings or private archive names here.

An additional archive's administrator is a research steward, not automatically the viewing anchor. Unless that owner also has a reviewed member-to-anchor person binding, responses use the named anchor's perspective, `/me.viewer.inTree` is false, and no chart card marks the anchor as the administrator. Owner controls and permitted findings remain available. A single known shared parent does not establish full or half siblings; the API withholds a precise collateral DNA expectation while the other parent is unknown.

`GET /archives` returns `{archives:[{id,title}],defaultArchive}` for the signed-in account, with no storage prefixes, account IDs or unauthorized archive titles. Literal `default` denotes the existing tree. Extra IDs are stable lowercase slugs (`^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,47}$`). Every existing request can select another authorized tree with `?archive=<id>`, including signing, files, notes and Listen audio. Extra archives have distinct non-overlapping storage prefixes and independent bundle, view, signing, notes and audio caches. Their explicit audience never inherits default-tree matching, ADMIN status or the Family feature pack. Invalid config fails closed for extra archives; revocation takes effect on the next request. Extra `/ask`, `/accounts` and `/match` refuse changes because bindings are managed in private configuration, so the legacy global account fields cannot overwrite another tree.

The web selector is populated only from this authorized catalog. Switching stops audio, closes the image viewer, drops pending responses, clears person/image/file caches and opens the selected archive's Home. Seen versions are scoped to the archive. An older backend returning 404 for `/archives` keeps the default experience. No additional archive or audience is activated by shipping this code; upload and verified private configuration are separate steps.

## Family archive tool and explicit research notes

`kade_family_history` is registered in the ordinary agent tool catalog. Its thin wrapper takes the authenticated request's actor (the server-resolved caller on a phone turn), reloads their account on every invocation, and dispatches to the same archive router without a network listener or loopback token. Missing or unresolved callers read nothing. Tool input cannot supply an actor, author, bucket key, arbitrary URL or fact mutation. Adding the tool to an agent grants no archive access.

Actions are `archives`, `search`, `person`, `media`, `findings`, `notes` and `save_note`. An omitted archive is chosen only if the authorized catalog has exactly one entry; otherwise the caller must select a listed archive. Every result includes the selected archive and `/me` account context. A steward with `viewer.inTree=false` / `perspectiveOnly=true` must never be spoken of as the viewing anchor. Person and media reads retain source citations, descriptions, evidence warnings and alternate/partial-source limitations. One known shared parent does not establish full siblings or an unknown second parent.

New `GET /research-notes` and `POST /research-notes` use the selected archive prefix. POST accepts only `{text (1–4,000 characters), personId?, userRequestedSave:true}` and returns `201 {ok,note}`. Notes are server-attributed `kind:unverified-recollection`, `status:needs-source-review`; the reviewed actual account person binding is retained, while an unbound steward has `author.personId:null`. An archive's matched readers and steward may submit a note; guests cannot. GET returns only the current author's notes, or all these notes for that archive's steward. Notes live under `<prefix>/research-notes/<id>.json` and are never written to bundles, records, parent links or facts. The existing owner-only `/notes` inbox is unchanged.

`save_note` additionally requires `user_requested_save:true`: only words the current user explicitly asks to save now. Do not backfill earlier chats or memory cards, copy another person's chat, or publish private research leads. A recollection is attributed testimony pending source review, never proof. Storage-write uncertainty tells the agent to check notes before retrying. Note reads and writes remain subject to current archive audience and revocation, separate from the general Family feature pack.

## The page (/family-history)
Server-rendered shell in the kade-pages style (SHARED_HEAD from kadePages.js, green buttons, 880px width, dark mode, focus ring, reduced motion), script `/assets/family/history.js?v=...` and styles `/assets/family/history.css`. Auth like the Library page: `getToken()` via `/api/auth/refresh`, redirect to `/login?redirect_to=/family-history` when signed out. It reads v2 answers for relationship pages; the authorized archive selector scopes every request. Sections (hash routes so Back works: `#/`, `#/tree/<id>`, `#/person/<id>`, `#/people`, `#/gallery`, `#/dna`, `#/note`, `#/notes`, `#/stories`, `#/story/<slug>`, `#/findings`, `#/accounts`): Home, Family tree (an SVG chart with a text version), Person, People, Stories, Research findings, and the owner's "Who can see this". Accessibility: one h1, real headings per section, `role="status" aria-live="polite"` for loading and errors, focus to the new view's heading, alt text on every image, a text equivalent for every chart, no auto-play, reduced motion and contrast respected, no sideways page scroll on a phone.
