Kiana, listening and speaking

Kiana’s portrait stays fixed while her mouth follows the recording. She blinks and makes small listening tilts. This is a development preview; call animation is not released yet.

Kiana has brown skin, textured locs with pink and dark heart charms, a cream knit top and layered heart necklaces.

The gesture study changes only the animation over the saved recording. It does not change the voice or claim to detect an emotion. In the call prototype, a leading voice direction selects the gesture when its audio clip starts.
