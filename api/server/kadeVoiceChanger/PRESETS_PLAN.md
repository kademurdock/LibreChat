# Voice Changer — preset brief (for the preset authors)

Kade: "so many settings, you could flick through them for a really long time
and stay entertained as hell from an audio perspective." She is a data hoarder
who is happy to scroll thousands. Quantity AND variety: no two presets in a
category should sound nearly the same (the fingerprint check flags those).

Targets: 60–120 hand-made presets per category (about 1,900 in all), plus the
generated Mix and match combinations (several thousand).

## Naming law (checked by tests and a reviewer)
- 2–6 plain words that say what you will hear: "Giant in a cave", "Walkie-talkie, low battery".
- No person names (real or fictional), no characters, no brands or trademarks, no
  product names, no slurs or mocking of disabilities, accents, ethnic groups or
  real medical conditions. Use descriptions: "Tin invader" not a TV robot's name;
  "Hard pitch correction" not a product; "Rotating speaker" not a brand;
  "1970s talking spelling toy" not its trade name; "8-bit console" not a company's console.
- Unique within a category; ids unique across everything and never changed later.
- `about`: one sentence for the ear, under 120 characters.
- `sub` groups 5–15 presets each so the list is browsable.

## An idea pool from Kade (Oct 22 2026)
Kade shared a design catalog another AI wrote for her: 1,094 named sound concepts in 61 families
(CORE_1094_SOUND_IDEAS.md, copy in the session scratchpad at zip-presets/). Use it as an idea pool:
borrow concepts that fit your categories, translate them into our modules (its "node chains" are
symbolic sketches, not our modules), and rename anything that breaks the naming law. Do NOT copy its
"ten variations" ladders (Hint, Classic, ...): intensity is our Effect strength macro, and near-
duplicates fail the fingerprint gate.

## Mix and match (generated)
`catalog.js` builds combinations from four lists: a voice (about 30 everyday/creature/robot
bases), a place (about 30 spaces), a device (about 25), and a motion (about 10). Generated
presets are named "<voice>, <place>" / "<voice> on <device>" / "<voice> <motion>", ids are
derived deterministically from the ingredient ids so they stay stable, and the list is browsable
by voice. Target: several thousand, all distinct.

## Quality gates every preset passes
- Validates against the registry (module ids, param names, ranges).
- Renders a 3 s synthetic voice and a real speech clip with no NaN, peak under the limiter's
  ceiling, length = input + tails.
- Fingerprint (mel-band statistics + stereo correlation + envelope) is not a near-duplicate of
  another preset in the same category.
- Speed: a 30 s render on the slowest chain under 6 s in Node.
