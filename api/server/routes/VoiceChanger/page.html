Everything runs on your own device and is free.

This page has five parts, each under its own heading: Sound, Presets, Fine-tune, Rack and Result. Pick a preset to change a voice in one step, or build your own rack of effects.

Sound

Press Record, talk or sing, then press it again to stop. Up to 3 minutes. Your microphone is recorded plain, with no noise cleaning, so the effects hear your real voice.

A file can be up to 8 minutes long, enough for a whole song.

Backing track

A second sound that plays under the changed voice, lined up from the very start, like the music of a song under its singer. It is mixed into every preview and into the full version. The effects never touch it.

In decibels, compared with the voice: 0 is as loud as the voice, minus 6 sits well under it.

No backing track.

Presets

Type a word like robot, cave or radio. Press Enter to go to the voices found.

Space plays or stops the preview. Enter makes the full version. Escape stops all sound.

Fine-tune

These change whichever voice you pick. Reset all tweaks puts them back.

The background is off while a backing track is playing, so the two do not fight. Untick the backing track in Sound to use a background.

Other sound

Some effects mix your voice with a second sound, so your words come out of a storm, a song or a guitar.

No other sound.

Rack

The effects the voice goes through, first to last. Each one is a group under its own heading, with its settings, then Move up, Move down, Bypass and Remove. A slider says its value in words; the number box beside it takes an exact value.
